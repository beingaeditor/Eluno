A little help getting together.

Connect your screens

Open Eluno on Windows, name this device, and choose “Connect another device.” Open Eluno on Android, tap “Scan computer invitation,” and scan the square code. Approve the request on Windows.

Share either way

Use “Add media” on the computer or “Add photos or videos” on Android. Play or view those items on either screen. Keep both apps open; Android sharing pauses when the app is in the background.

If it won’t connect

Use the same home Wi-Fi. Guest networks and some VPNs separate devices. If Windows asks, allow Eluno on private networks. Reopen Eluno after changing networks, then scan a fresh invitation. Never disable your firewall.

Your files and privacy

No account, cloud upload, or analytics. Only your chosen media is shared. “Allow copies” adds a convenient download button; it cannot prevent a viewer from recording received content. Stop sharing an item, remove a device, or disconnect to end future access.

Help with a connection

A connection report includes the app version and counts of devices, items, and pending requests. It excludes names, addresses, media, and invitation secrets. Nothing is sent automatically.
